PREAMBLE & USER AGREEMENT

MeoCut is engineered with a local-first, privacy-by-design architecture. We believe your video footage, creative projects, and personal media should remain strictly private on your physical device.

By accessing, downloading, or using MeoCut, you agree to the practices outlined in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the application.

SECTION 1: ACCOUNT-FREE ARCHITECTURE & LOCAL PROCESSING

1.1 No Account Required (Zero Registration)

MeoCut does not require users to create an account, register an email address, or log in with credentials. We do not collect, maintain, or store usernames, passwords, phone numbers, or social media profiles on any remote server. Because MeoCut maintains no cloud account database or identity records, there are no remote accounts or credentials to delete under Google Play account deletion mandates.

1.2 100% Local On-Device Processing (Zero Cloud Uploads)

All media processing operations—including video decoding, trimming, splitting, frame filtering, custom shaders, multi-track audio compositing, and on-device AI background segmentation (ONNX Runtime)—are executed 100% locally on your device’s physical hardware. We do NOT upload, transmit, stream, inspect, or store your photos, video clips, voice recordings, or exported video files on any remote servers, cloud infrastructure, or external databases.

1.3 Non-Destructive & Read-Only Media Access (EDL)

MeoCut accesses your device gallery files in a strictly read-only capacity to generate timeline previews via Edit Decision Lists (EDL). The Application never overwrites, modifies, or deletes your original source files.

SECTION 2: DEVICE PERMISSIONS & JUSTIFICATION

To perform standard video editing functions, MeoCut requests access to specific device capabilities:

2.1 Photos & Videos / Storage (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES)

Required exclusively to enable you to browse, pick, and import media into your editing timeline, and to write exported video files back to your device storage.

2.2 Microphone Permission (RECORD_AUDIO)

Requested only if you actively choose to record audio commentary or voiceovers directly onto the timeline tracks. We never access your microphone in the background.

2.3 Camera Permission (CAMERA)

Requested only if you choose to record video footage or snap photos directly within the App interface for immediate editing. We never access your camera in the background.

SECTION 3: ADVERTISING & THIRD-PARTY SDKS (ADS DATA)

3.1 Information Automatically Collected by Ad Networks

To support the ongoing free availability of MeoCut, the free tier integrates third-party mobile advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob). Ad networks may collect:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID on Android).
  • Hardware & OS Details: Device manufacturer, model, operating system version, system language, screen resolution, and mobile carrier.
  • Coarse Location & Network Metrics: IP address (used solely to approximate coarse geographic location for regional ad delivery) and connection type (Wi-Fi or cellular).
  • Ad Engagement Metrics: Ad impressions, click-through rates, and video view durations.

3.2 Opting Out of Targeted Advertising

You retain full autonomy to opt out of interest-based or targeted advertising at any time via your device operating system:

  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization.

SECTION 4: IN-APP SUBSCRIPTIONS & THIRD-PARTY SERVICE PROVIDERS

4.1 Zero Financial Data Collection

MeoCut does not collect, inspect, process, or store credit card numbers, debit card numbers, bank account details, UPI identifiers, or billing addresses. All subscription billing transactions are processed directly and securely by Google Play Billing.

4.2 Receipt & Entitlement Validation

MeoCut only receives an anonymized, encrypted purchase token or subscription receipt from Google Play (e.g., subscription product identifier, active entitlement validity, expiration timestamp) to unlock Pro features during your active subscription period.

4.3 Third-Party Privacy Links

Third-party SDKs integrated into MeoCut operate under their own independent privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

SECTION 5: CHILDREN'S PRIVACY (COPPA & GDPR-K COMPLIANCE)

5.1 Non-Collection of Children's Data

MeoCut is not directed toward children under thirteen (13) years of age (or under 16 years of age in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal identifiable information from children. If we become aware that personal data of a child has been inadvertently gathered, we will take immediate measures to permanently purge such data.

SECTION 6: USER RIGHTS, SECURITY & DATA ERASURE

6.1 Statutory User Rights (GDPR / CCPA / DPDP)

Depending on your geographic location (including the EU GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA, and India Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you have rights regarding your personal data. We will never deny services, charge differing prices, or provide degraded performance to users who exercise their statutory privacy rights.

6.2 Right to Complete Local Data Erasure

Because your project timelines, draft edits, and cache remain stored exclusively in your device’s sandbox, you can permanently erase all application data at any time by:

  1. Tapping Settings > Clear Cache inside MeoCut;
  2. Clearing App Storage via your device’s system settings; or
  3. Uninstalling the MeoCut application.

6.3 Data Security & Encryption in Transit

Any network communication initiated by integrated third-party SDKs (such as AdMob ad serving or Google Play purchase token confirmation) is strictly executed across encrypted industry-standard HTTPS / TLS protocols.
